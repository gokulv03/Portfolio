Outside firmware and control systems, I am drawn to the quiet art of photography. I process images in Adobe Lightroom, preferring underexposed frames and desaturated tones — a similar patience to debugging an interrupt handler.

I keep a Notion workspace as a second brain: coursework notes, project logs, and the slow progress of my German vocabulary toward B2. What drives me is the conviction that the most consequential problems live at the seam between physics and software.
